Collaboration

Communication Channels & Boundaries

Selecting the right medium ensures efficient alignment while respecting energy and context.

💬
Slack / Teams (Chat)
Best for non-mentally taxing topics, quick info exchanges, status updates, and lightweight syncs.
🗣️
Face-to-Face / Call
Best for complex, high-nuance, or easily misunderstood topics requiring real-time dialogue.
✉️
Email
Best for official decisions, formal documentation, or third-party / external communications.
📌 Context Upfront: Prefer context provided upfront, unless mutual understanding is already established.
🎯 Single-Threaded: Can handle only one live face-to-face or call conversation at a time for full focus.
Framework

Communication 3-Step Structure

For most proposals, discussions, or issue escalations, it is immensely helpful to structure information into 3 distinct steps:

STEP 1
Context
State facts only. Provide the background and current situation without subjective assumptions or premature conclusions.
➔
STEP 2
Problem Definition
Clearly define the core problem, why it matters, and its exact impact on the business, product, or team.
➔
STEP 3
Proposed Solutions
Offer concrete, actionable solutions or options (if available) with pros/cons to expedite decision-making.

Operating System

Mindset Quirks & Default Behaviors

Understanding my default working tendencies helps make our interactions smooth and predictable.

🗣️ Out-Loud Brainstorming
I brainstorm and explore multiple ideas out loud before honing in on the final solution. Don't take early draft thoughts as final decisions.
🧩 Coherence-Driven
I get easily irritated when logical gaps exist. I feel a strong need to sort out incoherence before effectively moving on to execution.
🔋 Topic-Selective Energy
I operate with peak energy when discussing product architecture & engineering topics; energy levels naturally vary in other areas.
🛠️ Build & Discover
I love building prototypes and running discovery in parallel, rather than waiting passively for fully baked requirements.
Debugging Guide

Known Bugs & How to Handle Them

Like any complex system, I have edge cases. Here are known behaviors and how you can support me:

🐛 Bug #1: Question Overload
Asking too many questions before committing
Support: Provide clear context upfront and explain relevance to the business, product, or team.
🐛 Bug #2: Slow Energy Recovery
Slow response time when running on low energy
Support: Give me time to recover. Feel free to explicitly nudge if it's an urgent matter!
🐛 Bug #3: Excitement Incoherence
Becoming incoherent when over-excited
Support: Give me a moment for ideas to sink in, and echo back what you hear to anchor alignment.
🐛 Bug #4: Over-Pushing
Pushing myself and others too hard
Support: Call it out! Explain if it's ineffective or if energy is better redirected elsewhere.
