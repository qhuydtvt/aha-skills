# A Manual of Huy

> A guide on how to work, communicate, and collaborate with me.

---

## 1. About Me
* **Name:** HuyNQ
* **Core Values:** Integrity, Winning Mindset, Continuous improvement
* **My Mission:** "Build useful things"

## 2. Communication Preferences
* **Preferred Channels:**
  * *Slack/Teams (Chat):* Best for non-mentally taxing and quick info exchange.
  * *Face-to-Face or Call:* Best for complex or easily misunderstood topics.
  * *Email:* Best for official decisions or third-party communications.
* **My Boundaries & Rules:**
  * *Context upfront:* I prefer context provided upfront, unless a mutual understanding has been established beforehand.
  * *One at a time:* I can only handle one face-to-face or live call conversation at a time.
  * *Preferred communication format:* For most matters, it would be a big help if they are communicated in 3 steps:
    1. Context (facts only)
    2. Problem definition
    3. Proposed solutions (if any)

## 3. How I Receive Feedback
* **Preferred Channel:** Face-to-face (does not matter if it is in private or public).
* **Preferred Feedback Format (3-Point Structure):**
  1. Context of the feedback.
  2. Establishing a shared understanding of the problem, how does it impact the business, the product or the team
  3. (optional) Proposed solution or improvement.

## 4. Quirks
* **My Default Behaviors:**
  * *Out-Loud Brainstorming:* I brainstorm and jump between ideas out loud before focusing on the final solution.
  * *Coherence-Driven:* I easily get irritated when things get incoherent, and I tend to sort them out (if they matter) before being able to move on.
  * *Topic-Selective Energy:* I tend to be more energetic when discussing product and engineering topics, and usually operate with lower energy in other areas.
  * *Build & Discover:* I love to build and run product discovery at the same time, rather than waiting for ideas to be fully baked.
* **My Bugs & How to Handle Them:**
  * *Bug:* I ask a lot of questions when a problem is not fully understood, sometimes too many, before making a commitment.
    * *Support:* Provide clear context and explain how it is relevant to the business, the product, or the team.
  * *Bug:* I respond slowly when running on low energy.
    * *Support:* Give me time to recover, still feel free to ask for faster response if its urgent matter.
  * *Bug:* I tend to be incoherent when getting over-excited.
    * *Support:* Give me some time to let things sink in, and echo back what you hear if possible.
  * *Bug:* I sometimes push myself and people too hard.
    * *Support:* Explain to me if it's not working or if our energy is better spent in other ways.

## 5. Support & Collaboration
* **Pet Peeves:**
  * Unscheduled communication for non-urgent matters.
  * Refusal of ideas or proposals without clear reasons.
* **My Golden Rules:**
  * Notify me beforehand for synchronous check-ins.
  * Give reasons for idea/proposal refusal, and optionally suggest alternatives.
